# BugNarrator Docs

**Narrate a whole testing session, anywhere on your Mac, and get back many filed
issues and a transcript you can search months later.**

That combination is the point, and it is what separates BugNarrator from the
clip-to-ticket tools:

- **A session, not a clip.** One exploratory pass produces *many* categorized
  draft issues with severity, component, and reproduction steps — not one ticket
  per recording.
- **Desktop-wide, not browser-bound.** Anything on your Mac is in scope: native
  apps, simulators, installers, terminal windows.
- **Local-first, genuinely.** Sessions live on your Mac. With the local Parakeet
  provider your audio never leaves the machine at all — no API key, no upload.
- **A searchable archive.** Every past session is full-text searchable, so the
  evidence outlives the sprint it came from.
